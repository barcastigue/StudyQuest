## Responsibilities
- **Project lead:** sprint goal, unblocking, final merge decision.
- **Board owner:** keeps Trello accurate, enforces the In Progress and Done rules.
- **Repo admin:** branch protection, invites, secrets hygiene.
- **Test lead:** verification evidence for review.

## Working agreements
- Daily 10-minute check-in (chat or call): done / next / blockers.
- A card moves only when its owner updates it.
- Every PR gets at least one reviewer who is not the author.
- Reviews answered within 24 hours.
