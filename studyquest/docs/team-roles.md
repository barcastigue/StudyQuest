## Responsibilities
- **Project lead (John):** sprint goal, unblocking, final merge decision.
- **Board owner (John):** keeps Trello accurate, enforces the In Progress and Done rules.

## Working agreements
- Daily 10-minute check-in (chat or call): done / next / blockers.
- A card moves only when its owner updates it.
- Every PR gets at least one reviewer who is not the author. With 3 members, rotate reviewers so everyone reviews.
- Reviews answered within 24 hours.
